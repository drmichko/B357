Classification of B(3,5,7) = RM(5,7)/RM(2,7)

This page reports the numerical experiments related to the classification of Boolean functions in B(3,5,7), which we computed in autumn 2026. They can be used to prove that the covering radius of RM(2,8) is smaller than 96, a result obtained during the M2 internship of Jean-Baptiste Nguyen.

Definitions

Let m be a positive integer. The space of Boolean functions from GF(2)^m into GF(2) is denoted by RM(k,m). This notation comes from coding theory, it is the Reed-Muller code of order k in m variables. The affine group AG(2, m) acts over the spaces RM(k,m), and thus on RM(k,m)/RM(r,m) when r ≤ k. Two Boolean functions f and g are said to be equivalent modulo RM(r, m), r-equivalent in short, if there exists an affine transformation A in AG(2,m) such that

g = f o A modulo RM(r,m)
where AG(2,m) is the affine group of order :
#AG(2, m) = 2^m (2^m - 1)(2^m - 2)...(2^m - 2^(m-1) )
it can be generated by three transformations : the shift S, a transvection T and a nontrivial translation U. The affine group acts over RM(k,m) and also over B(s,t,m) := RM(t,m)/RM(s-1,m). A formula for the rank n(s, t, m) of the action of AG(2,m) over B(s,t,m) was determined by Xiang-Dong Hou,
AGL(m, 2) Acting on R(r, m)/R(s, m), journal of algebra 171/3 (1995)
It satisfies :
n(s, t, m ) = n( m-t, m - s, m)

Notations

  • NL_2(f) the nonlinearity of order 2 of f, it is the minimum Hamming distance between f and RM(2,m).
  • B(s, t, m) denotes the quotient space of RM(t,m)/RM(s-1,m).
  • δ_a : indicator of the vector a
  • A coset f + RM(2,7) is an urcoset in NL_2(f+ δ_a) < NL_2 (f) for any a in GF(2)^m

Class numbers of B(s,t,7)

The objective of this numerical experiment consists in providing the classifications of B(3,5,7) = RM(5,7)/RM(2,7). An almost unreasonable task because the class number n(7,3,5) equals to 118140881980. One can use it to prove the covering radius of RM(2,8) is smaller than 96.

Methodology

Let us recall that we provides the classification of B(3,4,7), it is done in that project page. We use the descending methode starting from the classification of B(4,5,7). For an element h in B(4,5,7) whose 3-stabilizer is denoted by S, we use two ways to compute the classification of h + B(3,3,7) under the boundary action of S. The main issue is how to store the data... There are 179 classes in B(4,5,7), and we focus on those of degree 5. For each element h of B(4,5,7) identified by a number between 1 and 179, we provide a file text file rho/rho-{id} and a 2Go binary file data/{id}-3-3-7.zip that describe the orbit of h in a table of 2^32 bytes. An homogeneous cubic of B(3,3,7) is represented by 35-bit word. If the bit number i of the byte in T[j] is on then j*8 + i is a 35-bit integrer of a representative of h under its 3-stabilizer.

Application rho(2,8) < 96

We show that the covering radius of RM(2,8) cannot equal 96, that is, no Boolean function in 8 variables is at distance 96 from RM(2,8). The proof relies on an analysis of 7-variable functions of quadratic nonlinearity NL_2 = 40, which is the maximum possible in dimension 7.
